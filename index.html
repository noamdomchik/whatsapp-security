WhatsApp Security Settings

Follow these steps to protect your account

1

Enable Two-Step Verification

Open WhatsApp → Settings → Account → Two-Step Verification → Enter a 6-digit PIN you'll remember.

2

Check Active Sessions

WhatsApp Web → Check for unknown connected devices. If found, log out from all of them immediately.

3

Set Your Privacy

Settings → Privacy → Limit who can see your profile photo, status, and last seen to "My Contacts".

4

Don't Click Suspicious Links

Never open links from unknown people. This is the most common way accounts get hacked.

5

Update WhatsApp

Always keep the latest version of the app - updates include important security fixes.

6

Backup Your Chats

Settings → Chats → Chat Backup → Enable automatic backup to Google Drive or iCloud.

⚠️

If Your Account Is Hacked

1. Disconnect WhatsApp Web immediately
2. Contact WhatsApp support via: support@whatsapp.com
3. Tell your friends not to send money or personal info.
